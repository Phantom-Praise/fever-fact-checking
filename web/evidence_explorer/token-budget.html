How to interpret this experiment

This is an exploratory development-set experiment prompted by the Telemundo mistake. It is not an untouched final test. No training, retrieval changes or calibration were performed.

Saved 256-token probabilities provide the baseline. An independent 64-claim replay matched the baseline exactly. The 512-token condition runs the same evidence checkpoint with the exact saved evidence references; the combined model keeps its original fusion parameters and claim-only probabilities.

The checkpoint was trained with a 256-token limit. More input may add useful or distracting information. The results measure this setting change; they do not identify a human-readable cause for every prediction.

FEVER supplied the claims and human labels. The experiment and viewer were added with AI assistance. This page is local to this computer.
